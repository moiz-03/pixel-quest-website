Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy covers two things: the Pixel Quest website (the page you are reading) and the Pixel Quest Android app. They share nothing at runtime. The website is fully static and never talks to the app's backend.

1 · What the website collects

Nothing. The site sets no cookies. It loads no analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party fonts, no CDN scripts, and no chat widgets. Your IP address may appear in aggregate CDN access logs for operational purposes (rate limiting, abuse prevention); those logs are retained for no more than 30 days and are never tied to an individual visitor.

2 · What the app collects

Without an account: nothing leaves your device. Your quests, XP, streaks, and settings are stored locally in Android's app preferences.

With an account: the following data is written to Firebase Firestore, under a document readable only by you:

  • Your email address (from Firebase Authentication)
  • Your chosen display name
  • Quest titles, categories, XP values, and completion dates
  • Level, current XP, total XP, XP spent, streak length, streak history, number of streak freezes
  • A Firestore-assigned timestamp of the last update

Nothing else. No contacts, no location, no device identifiers, no advertising ID, no usage analytics.

3 · What the app does not collect

  • No location data
  • No contacts or calendar access
  • No camera or microphone access
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash reports or behavioural telemetry
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind

4 · Retention

Firestore data is retained for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes your Firestore data and your Firebase Authentication record immediately. Local data is cleared when you log out (session keys) or delete the app.

5 · Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: Settings → Delete account. Reauthentication with your password is required to prevent someone with an unlocked phone from wiping your data.

If you are in a jurisdiction with data-portability rights (for example, the EU under GDPR), you may request an export of your Firestore data by emailing hello@pixelquest.app. We will respond within 30 days.

6 · Third parties

The app uses Google Firebase for authentication and data storage. Firebase's own privacy practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. No other third party receives your data.

7 · Children

Pixel Quest is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

8 · Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will be updated and a note will appear in the changelog. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance.

9 · Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@pixelquest.app.
