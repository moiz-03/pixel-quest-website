A look inside

A preview of the app's main screens.

One quest a day

Pixel Quest is designed around a single daily check-in. Here's the loop.

Add a quest

Pick a habit. Give it a category and an XP value between 5 and 100.

Check it off

Tap to complete. XP lands on your level bar and into your streak.

Keep the streak

Miss a day and the streak resets, unless you spend 500 XP on a Streak Freeze.

Climb the ranks

Seven tiers, from Novice to Mythic. Every level asks for a little more XP than the last.
