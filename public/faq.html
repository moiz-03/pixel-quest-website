Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions we get most.

Is Pixel Quest free?

Yes. Free with no ads, no in-app purchases, no premium tier, and no upsell of any kind. The app is the whole product.

Does it need an account?

Yes. You create a free account (email and password) when you first open the app. Your quests are also kept on your device, so the app keeps working offline.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Pixel Quest is offline-first. Quests, XP, and streaks all work with no network connection. Changes sync the next time you're online.

Where is my data stored?

On your device (in unencrypted app preferences) and, because the app requires an account, in your private Firestore document, readable only by your signed-in user.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Settings, tap Delete account, and confirm your password. Your Firestore data and auth record are removed immediately. This cannot be undone.

Why isn't it on the Play Store yet?

Pixel Quest is in early access. The APK is published directly on this site until the Play Store listing goes live. Updates are posted to the changelog.

How do I install an APK?

Download the file, allow your browser to install unknown apps in Android settings, then tap the downloaded file. Step-by-step instructions, including troubleshooting, are on the download page.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Yes, to Firebase (Google) only: your account email, display name, quests, XP and streak data. No analytics, no ads, no other third party. This website collects nothing.

What is a streak freeze?

A streak freeze protects your global streak from one missed day. It costs 500 XP and you can own at most two at once. It is consumed automatically on the next cold start after you miss a day.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your global streak resets to zero — unless you own a streak freeze, in which case it is consumed and the streak survives. Two missed days in a row reset the streak even if you have freezes.
