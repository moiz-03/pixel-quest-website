What you can track

The full feature surface — every mechanic in the app, explained.

Quests

A quest is anything you want to do every day. "Morning run", "Read ten pages", "No sugar". Give it a title, a category, and an XP value between 5 and 100 — then check it off each day you do it.

Quests are yours alone. Nothing is shared, ranked, or compared against other users. The point is consistency, not competition.

XP & Levels

Every completion earns XP. Earn enough and you level up. The curve widens as you go: level 0 → 1 costs 100 XP, level 1 → 2 costs 150, level 2 → 3 costs 200, and so on. Formula: XP to next level = 100 + 50 × current level.

XP is also spendable — 500 XP buys a streak freeze — so your total and available balances are tracked separately.

Streaks

There are two streak concepts. A global streak bumps the first time you complete any quest on a given day. A per-quest streak counts consecutive days for that specific quest. Neither is required — you can use Pixel Quest as a plain checklist.

The global streak is the one that shows on the dashboard. Miss a day and it resets to zero — unless you spend a freeze.

Streak Freezes

A streak freeze protects your global streak from one missed day. If you miss yesterday and own at least one freeze, it's consumed automatically on the next cold start — the streak survives. Miss two days in a row and the freeze can't save you.

Price: 500 XP. Maximum owned at once: 2.

Reminders

Each quest can carry its own daily reminder time. Notifications are scheduled locally — no server, no push service, no third party. If you delete the quest, the reminder goes with it.

Offline-first

The app is offline-first: every quest, XP total, and streak value is mirrored locally before it touches the network. Add a quest on the subway — it syncs when you're back online.

Your data is mirrored on your device first, so the app keeps working with no signal. Your account syncs it to Firebase when you are back online.
