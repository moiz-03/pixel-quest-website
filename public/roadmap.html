Roadmap

Where Pixel Quest is headed next.

✅ Released (v1.0.0)

  • Core habit tracking with quests and XP.
  • Streaks and streak freezes.
  • Offline-first sync with Firebase.

🚧 In Progress (v1.1.0)

  • Home screen widgets for quick quest completion.
  • Customizable reminder sounds.
  • Performance improvements for older devices.

💡 Planned (v1.2.0 and beyond)

  • Weekly and monthly stat summaries.
  • Export data to CSV/JSON.
  • Integration with Google Fit / Apple Health.
